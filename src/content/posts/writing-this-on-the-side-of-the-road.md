Hey! This is my first post on this website, and I'm writing it outside. Like, *literally* on the side of the road.

Today was my roommate's birthday, so a bunch of us went out for dinner. We ended up having pizza at a shopping mall. Fun fact: this was only the second time I'd ever eaten pizza in my life. The first few slices were pretty good, but after a while it got a little too much for me, lol.

![](/images/pizza.jpg)

After eating, we wandered around the mall, browsed a bookstore, and eventually decided to head out. Well, *I* decided to head home. The others kept hanging out because I was already exhausted.

And that's when the first disaster happened: **I lost my parking ticket.** :)

I have absolutely no idea when it disappeared. I remember putting it in my pocket when the security guard handed it to me. But when I reached for it on my way out, my pocket was empty. I checked my pockets and backpack over and over, somehow still pretty calm, but there was nothing.

So a friend and I went looking for it. From the basement all the way up through floors one, two, three, four, five, six... Yeah, we knew how pointless that was. The chances of finding that tiny ticket were probably lower than the chances of a planet crashing into Earth.

But then we saw a sign:

> **LOST PARKING TICKET: 100,000 VND FINE.**

Now, maybe 100,000 VND doesn't sound like much to you. But to a broke student who sometimes has to skip meals, that's a *lot* of money.

An hour passed. We gave up. I was tired, and I'd made peace with losing the money. Except there was one small problem: **I didn't even have 100,000 VND in cash.** Neither did my friend. Between the two of us, we had a little over 80,000 VND, lol.

The security guard wouldn't accept a bank transfer, so I went around asking people if I could transfer them money in exchange for cash. For some reason, a few people looked genuinely annoyed or judgmental instead of just saying no. Maybe they thought I was trying to scam them? I don't know.

Thankfully, one man agreed to help. After what felt like 7749 forms and procedures, I finally got my beloved motorbike back.

*Thank God. I could finally go home.*

Or so I thought.

On the way back, **my motorbike ran out of gas.** I ended up pushing it for a few kilometers, trying to find a gas station. It was getting late; some stations were closed, and others had run out of gas themselves. Eventually, I was so damn tired that I dragged the bike onto the sidewalk and just sat there, completely defeated.

I pulled out my phone. And of course, its battery was dead too. :)))

So I took out my laptop, connected my phone to it, and charged it just enough to try calling some friends. Except I'd also run out of mobile data *and* phone credit. Amazing. I ended up wandering around again, this time trying to get Wi-Fi from a nearby pharmacy.

After failing to reach a few friends, I finally called an older friend of mine. Even though I was pretty far from where he lived, he still came to get me. It took him about thirty minutes. According to him, that wasn't very far. According to me, it absolutely was.

And while I was sitting there waiting for him, I started writing this.

Yep. My very first blog post is being written while I'm exhausted, frustrated, and stranded on the side of the road. So if the writing is all over the place, now you know why. (Bruh, I'm really just making excuses for being bad at writing.) But whatever. Good or bad, I'm posting it anyway.

![](/images/1792853953462_5501492539153734889_5501492539153734889_10c5724984aacf9e67a3a386e00cc0b0.jpg)

That was me at the time. Looking back, it's kind of ridiculous because there were definitely better ways to handle the situation. :v But hey, without all of that, this post probably wouldn't exist.

Oh, and a huge shout-out to **H, the absolute GOAT**, for coming to save me.

---

**Update: I made it home. Unfortunately, the story wasn't over.**

I was actually about to publish this post, but what the hell? I swear I must be cursed.

After getting home, I went out to a coffee for a drink. When I came back to get my motorbike, I noticed a little note tucked onto it.

![](/images/1792979146094_114543838515519014_2955566228386833588_cc76b77bac2b107838c98d01272b97a4.jpg)

At first, I had no idea what it meant. I looked around, confused, but nothing seemed out of place. I figured maybe someone was messing with me. After standing there for a while, I decided to put on my helmet and leave.

The moment I picked it up, **it literally fell apart into five pieces.** =))))

And suddenly, I understood the note. I still have no idea how that even happened. It was a fairly new full-face helmet, too. I was annoyed, obviously, but I figured whoever did it probably didn't mean to. I spent a while trying to piece it back together well enough to wear, got home, and slept until the afternoon.

And... that's it. Thanks for reading this far.

Honestly, I have no idea what I'll post here next. I'm not a particularly good writer, and I'm usually pretty hesitant to share my personal life online, especially the less cheerful parts of it. So maybe the next post will be something a little more technical. Who knows?

Anyway, thanks again for reading.
